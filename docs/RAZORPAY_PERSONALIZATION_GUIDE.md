# Razorpay Personalization & Audience Connection Guide

This guide outlines how to leverage your new re-enrollment system and Razorpay's native features to create a high-conversion, "premium" experience for your returning students.

---

## 1. Dashboard Visual Branding (Set Once)
The first impression matters. Students should feel like they never left your website when they click the payment link.

**Action Steps in Razorpay Dashboard:**
1.  **Brand Color**: Go to **Settings > Account Settings**. Set your Brand Color (e.g., your signature gold or navy). This color will appear on the "Pay Now" buttons.
2.  **Logo**: Upload your professional logo. It will appear at the top of every payment link.
3.  **Support Details**: Ensure your support email and phone number are updated. This builds trust if a student has a payment query.

---

## 2. Dynamic Code Personalization (Active)
Our system already automates the most important parts of personalization:

*   **Zero-Form Checkout**: By passing `name`, `email`, and `contact` to the API, students skip the entire data-entry phase. They click "Pay" and go straight to the UPI/Card selection.
*   **The "Welcome Back" Greeting**: We've updated the description to include the student's name dynamically.
    *   *Example*: `Hi Sarah! Welcome back to the June 2026 Advanced Batch.`

---

## 3. Copywriting for Connection
The "Description" field in Razorpay and the "Subject Line" in your Resend emails are your most powerful tools for connection.

### Strategy: The "Inner Circle" Vibe
Instead of: `"Your enrollment link for June"`
Use: `"Welcome back, [Name]! Your spot for June is reserved."`

**Tips for descriptions:**
*   **Acknowledge the Journey**: Use phrases like "Continuing your progress" or "Leveling up in June."
*   **Address the "Why"**: Briefly mention one key benefit (e.g., "Join us for 10 live sessions on advanced techniques").

---

## 4. Operational Engagement Strategies
To maximize the "Audience Connection," consider these manual follow-up workflows:

### The "Personal Touch" (WhatsApp)
If a student hasn't paid after 48 hours:
1.  Open the **Invitation Logs** in your Admin Dashboard.
2.  Filter for **"Sent"** (Unpaid).
3.  Click the **Copy icon** next to the student's name.
4.  Send a quick WhatsApp: *"Hey [Name]! Just wanted to make sure you got the invite for the next batch. Here is your direct link if you need it: [Paste Link]"*

### The "Urgency" Lever
Your links currently expire in **7 days**. Mention this in your communications to encourage students to secure their spot before the link expires and the slot goes to a new student.

---

## 5. Advanced: Custom Domains
As your audience grows, you can request a **Custom Domain** from Razorpay support.
*   **Standard**: `rzp.io/l/yourlink`
*   **Premium**: `pay.aishwaryamanikarnike.com/june-batch`
*   *Benefit*: Total brand consistency and higher trust.
